Paper Phase Transition: interactive teaching lab¶

Purpose: learn how an illustrative business-dependence story becomes an Ising/QUBO/circuit model, then compare exact, classical, and cached quantum sampling evidence. This notebook runs locally in qBraid Lab; it never starts a server, authenticates, or submits a remote job. Status: not deployed to qBraid Lab.

Use the tabs in order. Predict before revealing each answer. Coefficients are challenge-suggested illustrations, not forecasts or causal estimates.

Notation and model card¶

Expand the math and glossary
  • Node: industry in one region. A tie is mutual dependence, not causal proof.
  • Resistance $r_i$: illustrative friction to digital adoption.
  • Pressure $A(t)$: an illustrative external push; equilibrium is not an adoption-delay or switching-cost model.
  • Degeneracy: more than one exact lowest-energy bitstring is a legitimate ambiguity. It creates a flip-year interval, not a numerical error.
  • Fragility: a small gap can make a result sensitive; a nonzero energy error remains an error even when flagged as fragile.
