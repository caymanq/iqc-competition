The difference in expectation value (-2.926 vs -2.673) reflects optimizer stochasticity: COBYLA is a local optimizer and can converge to different local minima depending on the landscape it explores. Run 1 benefited from a better-conditioned starting point (warm-started through 7 prior years of optimization), which is why it found a deeper minimum. This is a concrete demonstration of why warm-starting matters for QAOA.
